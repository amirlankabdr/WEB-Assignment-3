Computer parts

Three parts you will see in most PC specifications.

Processor

The processor, or CPU, carries out instructions. It is important for running programs and completing calculations.

RAM

RAM temporarily stores data that programs are using. More RAM can help when working with several programs at once.

Storage

Storage keeps your files and programs. An SSD usually loads the operating system and applications faster than a hard drive.

Types of PCs

Different tasks need different hardware.

Study PC

Used for assignments, video calls, browsing, and basic programming.

  • Entry-level or mid-range CPU
  • Enough RAM for your programs
  • SSD for files and applications

Integrated graphics may be enough.

Gaming PC

Used for games. The parts you need depend on the games and screen resolution.

  • CPU suited to your games
  • Dedicated graphics card
  • Enough storage for game files

Check the requirements of your games.

Editing PC

Used for editing photos, videos, and other demanding projects.

  • CPU suited to editing software
  • Extra RAM for larger projects
  • Fast storage and backup space

Hardware needs depend on your software.
